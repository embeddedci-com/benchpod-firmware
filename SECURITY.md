# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for a security problem. Email
[hello@embeddedci.com](mailto:hello@embeddedci.com?subject=Security) with "Security" in the
subject. Include what you found, how to reproduce it and what it affects, and give us a chance to
fix it before you make it public.

- Policy and what to expect after a report:
  https://www.embeddedci.com/docs/benchpod-security#reporting-a-vulnerability
- Contact in machine-readable form: https://www.embeddedci.com/.well-known/security.txt

We reply within two business days and keep you posted until it is fixed. Test only against your
own BenchPod and your own account.

## Supported versions

Only the latest release gets security fixes.
